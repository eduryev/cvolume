Amsterdam property prices, by neighbourhood

Historical growth in sale price per m² across Amsterdam's seven boroughs (2016–2026), and what a €700,000 purchase today could be worth in 5 and 10 years if each area keeps compounding at its recent pace.

How to read this. Current (2025–2026) price levels and district ranking come from published broker and municipal reporting (see sources below). The full year-by-year 2016–2026 series and the 5-/10-year growth rates are a model reconstruction anchored to those published figures and to Amsterdam's well-documented market cycle (2015–19 boom, 2019–21 plateau, 2022–23 rate-driven correction, 2024–26 recovery) — live access to the official granular dataset (Gemeente Amsterdam Onderzoek & Statistiek, CBS StatLine) was not reachable from this session's network. Treat the shapes and rankings as directionally sound and the 5-/10-year projections as illustrative, not as a substitute for the primary data or financial advice.
